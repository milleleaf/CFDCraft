# Notice

The reference numbers in `data/dvd_table5.toml` are the bench mark solution of Table V (page 256) of G. de Vahl Davis, "Natural convection of air in a square cavity: a bench mark numerical solution", Int. J. Numer. Meth. Fluids 3, 249-264 (1983), DOI 10.1002/fld.1650030305, published by John Wiley and Sons.

The paper states no terms for its numbers. They are quoted here as facts, with the citation, and only the numbers that the comparison needs are kept. No page, figure or file of the paper is included.

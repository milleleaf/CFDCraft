# Notice

The reference numbers in `data/st_2d2.toml` are the estimated intervals for the exact solution of the equations that M. Schafer and S. Turek published, with F. Durst, E. Krause and R. Rannacher, for the flow around a cylinder at Reynolds number 100 (benchmark 2D-2): "Benchmark Computations of Laminar Flow Around a Cylinder", in Flow Simulation with High-Performance Computers II, Notes on Numerical Fluid Mechanics 52, Vieweg, 1996, pp. 547-566, Table 4, page 13 (DOI 10.1007/978-3-322-89849-4_39).

The paper states no terms for its numbers. Only the eight bounds and the definitions the comparison needs are quoted, as facts, with the citation. No text, table layout or figure of the paper is copied.

The widened gate around the intervals is a setting of this project. It is not part of the published result.

# Notice

This crate holds reference numbers for two backward-facing step benchmarks. It holds numbers only: no figure, page or data file of any source is redistributed.

The turbulent step is compared with the experiment of D. M. Driver and H. L. Seegmiller, "Features of a reattaching turbulent shear layer in divergent channel flow", AIAA Journal 23(2), 163-171, 1985, DOI 10.2514/3.8890. The reattachment length and the mean-velocity profiles at four stations were taken from the NASA Turbulence Modeling Resource page for the two-dimensional backward-facing step (https://tmbwg.github.io/turbmodels/backstep_val.html), maintained by Ethan Vogel and Clark Pederson. The page states no terms of use, so only the numbers needed are kept, each with its source.

The laminar step is compared with the experiment of B. F. Armaly, F. Durst, J. C. F. Pereira and B. Schonung, "Experimental and theoretical investigation of backward-facing step flow", Journal of Fluid Mechanics 127, 473-496, 1983, DOI 10.1017/S0022112083002839. The reattachment lengths are the readings of a published figure (Figure 4, cross-checked against Figure 14), made by eye against the figure's axes, with the reading uncertainty stated in the data file. No figure or page is kept. The copy of the paper that the readings were made from was supplied for this purpose, and its terms of use were not stated in it.

The source of every number, how it was obtained and its checksum are listed in `data/PROVENANCE.md`.
